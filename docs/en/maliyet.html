Where the prices came from

  • The report gives only two totals per product and no part prices. Here each main part carries its own distributor price. What is left of the report's total once those are taken out is the "other" line: power conversion, protection, connectors and the enclosure. It comes out the same for all three broadcast units, as it should for the same board, and that is what says the breakdown agrees with the report.
  • The price at a hundred uses the report's own discount from one to a hundred. No seller's tier at a thousand could be read, so a thousand is assumed to cost 90 % of a hundred; that is the one assumption in the bill.
  • The part prices are the distributor prices a web search returned on 23 September 2026. The network rules of the environment this was done in did not allow opening the seller pages, so no price tier was read off a page directly. The links are above; if a price is wrong, one line in bom.toml fixes it and this page and the table follow.

One unit's yearly consumption3 kWh/yearworked out: SX1280 and STM32G0 datasheets, this project's arithmeticOne broadcast unit's yearly consumption. It was 35 kWh, a steady 4 W, ten times too much for an SX1280 board. The unit spends nearly all its time listening: the SX1280 draws a few mA doing that and the microcontroller one or two, under 30 mW together at 3,3 V. With the mains converter's idle loss and the replies it sends, the average stays under 0,34 W, which is 3 kWh a year. That is a generous upper bound.
Cellular data line, a year0 TL/yeardesign choice: this project, as a design choiceWhat a year of data costs an anchor with no line of its own: nothing, because no unit carries a SIM card. The report says nothing of the kind; the first cost model (ADR-0006) assumed a cellular plan per anchor and it has been taken out (ADR-0079). The tie to the centre runs through the tunnel's own communications spine in a tunnel and a signalised junction's cabinet in town. The other units are watched by the phones and vehicle receivers that range against them: those already know which unit answered, so a unit that stops answering shows up without a line of its own.
Signalised junction spacing2 anchorsdesign choice: this project, as a deployment choiceHow much of the grid stands at a signalised junction: every second spot of every second row, so a quarter. A town's main streets are signalised, and a signalised junction already carries a controller cabinet: mains for the heads and a line to the traffic management centre. An anchor there does what the municipality's cameras and detectors already do, takes its link from that cabinet, and buys no data plan of its own. The structure, the height and the fitting cost are the same lighting column, so nothing about the geometry moves. A quarter is the conservative reading: half of the main-street junctions in Ankara's centre is defensible too, and saves twice as much.
A unit's service life10 yearsstandard: Turkish Revenue Administration depreciation list, 3.49.4: radio telephone, telegraph or broadcast receiving devices and systems, 10 years, 10 %How many years a unit lasts before it is replaced. It was 8, an assumption. An anchor is a radio system, and the tax rules give that 10 years. The positioning line (3.49.8, 5 years) is for devices that locate by satellite, which an anchor does not (ADR-0089).
Maintenance visits a year0,20 visits/yeardesign choice: this project, as an operating choiceHow often an anchor is visited a year. It was 0,5, a calendar visit every two years. But the central system already sees every unit's status (the report: the units' records are monitored). A crew goes when one fails rather than by the calendar. One failure in five years is a pessimistic figure for a sealed board.
